# Risk List and Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tasks are late | Medium | High | Small tasks, clear owners, daily status check | Project lead |
| AI invents details | Medium | High | Verify dates, facts and costs against source | QA |
| Team misses meeting | Medium | Medium | Share notes and action items in repository | Documentation lead |
| Private data is shared | Low | High | Use fictional/sample data; remove personal details | All |
| Automation fails | Medium | Medium | Check Actions run manually and keep summary template | Project lead |
